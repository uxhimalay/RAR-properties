## UX and security sweep (same day, after the card fixes)
Two faults were reported from screenshots and both turned out to be classes of bug, so the whole page was scanned for more at 390, 834 and 1440.

**Fixed**
| Finding | Fix |
|---|---|
| The deal's room row asked for four photos when the property had three, so the open photo grew past the row and spilled out of its cell. | The row never asks for more photos than it has, and a single photo simply fills it. |
| Category cards changed size per tab: a row with two listings stretched them, so warehouse cards were half as wide again as residential ones, with mismatched photo shapes. | The card size comes from the row's width alone, so it is identical on every tab; the enquiry panel absorbs the leftover width. |
| Details only appeared on the hovered card, so most cards were bare photos. | Every card now carries the same block, always visible: status chip, name, community and type, price and size. Photos sit in a fixed box, so shape no longer matters. |
| A thin category ended on a large empty outline. | The enquiry panel has a title, a line and the link, all editable in the admin, and clamps its text. |
| Small text links were 13 to 25px tall, under any touch guidance. | An invisible hit area (`hit-slop`) grows them to about 44px without moving anything, with a little real padding where needed. All controls now pass at 390 and 1440. |
| Controls that removed their focus outline showed nothing when tabbed to. | One rule gives every such control a gold focus ring. |
| Service cards and the enquiry panel could spill if copy ran long. | Both clip and clamp their lines. |
| The footer's first line landed under the fixed navbar when reached from the menu. | Anchors can ask for room with `data-anchor-offset`; the footer asks for 64px. |
| On phone, the footer's stacked links sat close enough for their tap areas to overlap. | The rows are spaced further apart. |

**Checked and left alone:** the roll-label pattern (two stacked copies in a clipped box) and screen-reader-only text both register as "clipped" in a naive scan but are deliberate. No horizontal page overflow, no duplicate ids, no focusable content inside `aria-hidden`, no text under 10px, and no unnamed controls at any size.

**Security** is written up in docs/SECURITY.md and was probed end to end: headers present, unauthenticated reads and writes refused, cross-site writes refused, ten wrong passcodes locking an address out, a PHP file disguised as a PNG refused, SVG refused, traversal names neutralised, deletes confined to the uploads folder, a 9 MB document refused, the image optimiser refusing a foreign URL, and forged or expired cookies refused.


## Listings page (same day)
The category row was a shortlist with no way to reach the rest, so `/properties` was added:
- `/properties` lists every live property; `/properties/<category>` lists one, so a category can be linked and indexed (each has its own title and description). An unknown category is a 404.
- Filters: status, bedrooms, budget, and an order (featured, price either way, largest, recently updated), with a live count and "Clear filters". Twelve at a time, then "Show more".
- The enquiry panel that ends the home row ends this grid too, and becomes the empty state when the filters match nothing.
- The link beside the tabs reads "See all N <category>", with the count from the live listings; its wording and the page's copy are admin fields.
- The property page's back link now goes to its category rather than the home page.
- The navbar gained a `solid` mode: on a page that does not open on a dark photo its white type was invisible until you scrolled.
Verified at desktop and phone: the link and count, filtering, sorting, the empty state, clearing, category switching, "Show more", a card opening its property, the back link, a 404 for an unknown category, no horizontal overflow, no failing tap targets, and a clean console.
